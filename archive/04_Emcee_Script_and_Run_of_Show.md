# 🎤 04: Emcee (MC) Script & Run of Show (Teen Edition: Ages 13–17)

**Host Persona:** Dynamic, articulate, charismatic, collegiate. Treats participants as young adults and future university peers, balancing sharp humor with clear time discipline.  
**Target Audience:** Secondary School Students (Ages 13–17, mixed gender).

## 2. Word-for-Word Emcee Script

### [08:30 – 09:00 AM] Arrival & Vibe Setting
*(Modern synthwave/lofi background music playing. Emcee speaks with warm, natural energy)*
> **Emcee:**  
> *"Good morning, everyone! Welcome to Universiti Teknologi Malaysia and our **NextGen Tech Summit**!  
> If you've just walked in, make your way over to the registration counter to grab your official delegate badge. Check the color on your badge to locate your startup team table.  
>  
> At each table, you have an awesome UTM student mentor waiting to welcome you. Take this time to introduce yourselves, exchange names, and grab a seat. We’re kicking off our opening session promptly at 9:00 AM. Make yourselves at home!"*

---

### [09:00 – 09:15 AM] Opening Address & Icebreaker
> **Emcee:**  
> *"Good morning, future tech pioneers and creators! How is the energy in the room today? Let me hear Table 1! Table 3? Table 6 at the back? That's what I'm talking about!  
>  
> My name is **[Emcee Name]**, and on behalf of the organizing team, it is an absolute privilege to welcome all of you to today’s summit.  
>  
> Look around you: You represent the generation that doesn't just use technology—you were born into it. But there’s a massive difference between passively scrolling through algorithms and actually understanding how they are built, how they affect you, and how to harness them to build something world-changing. Today is about making that jump from consumer to creator.  
>  
> Before we invite our keynote speaker, let’s warm up those synapses with our **Icebreaker Battles**! We have two quick rounds: **Teen Tech Charades** and the **Emoji Tech Decoder**. Table points are on the line, and trust me, every point counts toward the final championship! Mentors, get your scorecards ready. Table 1, send up your first actor!"*  
>  
> *(Facilitates Charades for 8 mins, then runs Emoji Tech Decoder for 7 mins)*  
>  
> *"Incredible intuition out there! Give yourselves a huge round of applause!"*

---

### [09:15 – 09:35 AM] Keynote Introduction: Tech Talk
> **Emcee:**  
> *"Now, let's get down to business. In the last 18 months, Artificial Intelligence has gone from science fiction to writing essays, generating cinema-quality video, and diagnosing diseases. But how does an LLM actually predict what to say? Why does your TikTok feed seem to know you better than your closest friends?  
>  
> To pull back the curtain on AI, algorithmic engines, and what this means for your future careers, please give a warm, energetic welcome to our keynote speaker, **[Speaker Name & Title]**!"*

*(Speaker delivers 20-minute talk)*

---

### [09:35 – 10:00 AM] Fast-Answer Tech Showdown: “Siapa Cepat, Dia Menang!”
> **Emcee:**  
> *"A massive thank you to [Speaker Name] for that incredible breakdown!  
>  
> Now, it’s time to see who has the sharpest tech instincts in the room. Dan berita baik: Korang tak perlu buka telefon bimbit langsung! Semua soalan akan keluar besar-besar di skrin utama di hadapan!  
>  
> Ini adalah **Fast-Answer Tech Showdown: Siapa Cepat, Dia Menang!** ⚡  
>  
> Peraturannya senang:  
> 1. Saya dan abang AV akan paparkan soalan dengan 4 pilihan jawapan (A, B, C, D).  
> 2. Semua tangan kekal atas meja dulu... Jangan angkat selagi saya belum sebut: **'3, 2, 1... ANGKAT TANGAN!'**  
> 3. Fasilitator dan saya akan spot siapa yang angkat tangan paling laju!  
> 4. Siapa jawab betul, mejanya dapat **+20 Mata**, dan penjawab terus dapat **hadiah token segera**! Kalau salah, meja lain boleh rampas!  
>  
> Ada 5 soalan berapi menanti korang. Meja 1 sampai Meja 7, are you ready?! Let's lock in!"*  
>  
> *(Emcee membaca 5 soalan bergilir-gilir dengan penuh tenaga, disusuli reveal jawapan dan penerangan 30 saat)*  
>  
> *(Selesai Soalan 5 - Skor Meja)*:  
> *"Fuh! Sangat laju dan tajam minda korang semua hari ini! Tahniah kepada meja-meja yang berjaya mengutip mata terpantas dalam Fast-Answer Showdown sebentar tadi! Papan markah menunjukkan persaingan sangat sengit!"*

---

### [10:00 – 10:15 AM] Refreshment & Networking Break
> **Emcee:**  
> *"Great work on the quiz, everyone! It’s now 10:00 AM, and your processors need a recharge.  
>  
> We have fresh refreshments and drinks served at the foyer. Feel free to use the restrooms down the corridor to your right, and take a moment to chat with your UTM student mentors about university life, coding, and campus projects.  
>  
> We will resume sharp at **10:15 AM** for our high-impact Cyber Resilience session. Enjoy your break!"*

---

### [10:15 – 10:45 AM] Session 2: “Be Smart Online” (Cyber Resilience)
> **Emcee:**  
> *"Welcome back, everyone! Please take your seats.  
>  
> When we think of cyber threats, we often picture movie hackers in dark hoodies hacking into banks. But in 2026, the primary targets of sophisticated phishing, credential stuffing, AI voice clones, and social engineering are actually students and teens.  
>  
> How do you protect your identity? How do you ensure what you post today won’t haunt your university scholarship or career five years from now? And how do we deal with toxic online culture and blackmail?  
>  
> To guide us through building real digital armor, please welcome our cyber resilience specialist, **[Speaker/Trainer Name]**!"*

*(Speaker delivers 30-minute workshop, including table scenario dilemma)*

---

### [10:45 – 11:30 AM] The AI Startup Sprint: “The Pitch Challenge”
> **Emcee:**  
> *"Thank you, [Speaker Name]! Essential knowledge every single one of us needs in our pocket.  
>  
> Now, it is time for the highlight of the summit: **The AI Pitch Challenge**! 🚀  
>  
> For the next 45 minutes, forget that you are high school students. You are the **Founding Team of a Tech Startup**.  
>  
> Each table has a clean Mahjong paper canvas, markers, and sticky notes. Your challenge is to invent an AI-powered solution to a real-world problem faced by students, schools, or our community.  
>  
> Your poster pitch deck needs 4 key elements:  
> 1. **Startup Name, Logo & Slogan**  
> 2. **The Problem & Who Suffers From It**  
> 3. **Your AI Engine & Top 3 Features** (with an app UI wireframe sketch)  
> 4. **Your Business or Social Impact Model**  
>  
> At 11:30 AM, each team will have **strictly 3 minutes** on stage to pitch to our panel of judges. We have awards for *Best Innovation*, *Best UX Design*, *Venture Pitch Masters*, and more!  
>  
> Mentors, start the brainstorming! You have 45 minutes on the clock—go make history!"*

---

### [11:30 – 11:50 AM] Startup Pitches & Judging Panel
> **Emcee:**  
> *"Time is up, founders! Cap your markers and step back!  
>  
> Welcome to the **NextGen Startup Pitch Arena**! Our judges are seated and ready.  
> Remember the pitch rules: You have exactly 3 minutes. When you hear the single chime at 2:00 minutes, you have 60 seconds to close strong. When you hear the double buzzer, your time is up. Make sure both your male and female co-founders share the stage!  
>  
> Kicking off our pitch session with energy... please welcome **Team 1**!"*  
>  
> *(Smoothly rotates through all teams, keeping time with enthusiasm and encouraging applause)*  
>  
> *(After all pitches finish)*:  
> *"Give yourselves a massive round of applause! The creativity, the technical depth, and the stage presence we saw today was simply world-class!"*

---

### [11:50 AM – 12:00 PM] Awards, Closing & Grand Photo
> **Emcee:**  
> *"And now, the moment we've all been waiting for—the Honorary Awards!*  
>  
> *Our judging panel has deliberated, and it's time to announce the winners of each category. When your team name is called, please stand up proudly at your table!*  
>  
> *(Announces honorary titles with dramatic flair — no physical prizes, just pure recognition and applause):*  
> *🦄 The Unicorn Award for Best Innovation goes to... Team [X]!*  
> *🎨 Best Visual Pitch Deck goes to... Team [Y]!*  
> *🎤 Venture Pitch Masters for Best Stage Delivery goes to... Team [Z]!*  
> *🛡️ Cyber Ethics & Social Impact Champion goes to... Team [A]!*  
> *⚙️ Technical Feasibility Award goes to... Team [B]!*  
>  
> *And our Fast-Answer Quiz Champions with the fastest reflexes today... Team [C]! Give them a standing ovation!*  
>  
> *Seterusnya, sebagai tanda setinggi-tinggi penghargaan dan terima kasih daripada mahasiswa Universiti Teknologi Malaysia kepada Asrama Darul Falah, pihak kami berbesar hati ingin menyampaikan Bingkai Penghargaan Khas kepada wakil pengurusan ASDAF! Dijemput **[Nama Wakil Staf ASDAF]** naik ke pentas!*  
>  
> *(Penyampaian Bingkai Penghargaan kepada staf ASDAF)*  
>  
> *Congratulations to every single innovator here today! You've proven that brilliant ideas don't need big budgets—they just need passionate minds working together.*  
>  
> *Before we wrap up, let's assemble at the stage front with your pitch posters for our **Official Summit Photo**! Mentors, help guide your teams forward.*  
>  
> *On the count of three, everyone give your best power pose and shout: **'BUILD THE FUTURE!'***  
>  
> *1... 2... 3... **BUILD THE FUTURE!** 📸*  
>  
> *Thank you so much for joining us! Have a safe journey back to ASDAF, and keep innovating!"*
