# 🖥️ 02: Session Content & Slide Outlines (Teen Edition: Ages 13–17)

This document provides updated slide outlines, visual recommendations, and speaker talking points tailored specifically for secondary school students (Ages 13–17, mixed gender).

---

## Part 1: Tech Talk — “Technology Around Us: AI, Algorithms & What Lies Ahead”
- **Duration:** 20 Minutes (09:15 – 09:35 AM)
- **Target Audience:** Teens aged 13–17
- **Tone:** Intellectual, forward-looking, conversational, treating students as future tech builders.

### Slide-by-Slide Outline & Script

| Slide # | Slide Title | Visual / Media Suggestion | Talking Points & Crowd Engagement |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Slide 1** | **Beyond the Screen: The Invisible Code Shaping Your World** | Sleek dark-mode aesthetic with neon glowing neural network graphic. | **Speaker:** *"How many of you opened Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, or asked ChatGPT a question this morning? We spend 6+ hours a day inside digital algorithms. Today, we look behind the curtain to understand the code that influences what you see, buy, and think."* |
| **Slide 2** | **The Generative AI Revolution** | Logos of OpenAI (ChatGPT), Midjourney, Claude, Gemini, and GitHub Copilot. | **Speaker:** *"In 2022, AI shifted from analyzing data to creating data. Computers can now generate human-level essays, photorealistic art, and working Python code in milliseconds. It’s the biggest technological shift since the birth of the Internet."* |
| **Slide 3** | **How Large Language Models Actually "Think"** | Graphic showing next-word prediction probability tree (`"The cat sat on the [mat: 85%, roof: 10%]"`) | **Speaker:** *"AI doesn't have a soul or human consciousness. At its core, an LLM is a hyper-sophisticated prediction engine. It predicts the most mathematically plausible next word based on reading billions of web pages."* |
| **Slide 4** | **The Algorithm Behind Your FYP (For You Page)** | Diagram of the recommendation feedback loop: Watch Time + Rewatch + Comments ➔ Dopamine loop. | **Speaker:** *"Why can you scroll TikTok or Reels for 3 hours without noticing? The algorithm tracks the exact millisecond you pause on a video. Your attention is the currency tech giants compete for. Knowing how it works helps you control it, instead of it controlling you."* |
| **Slide 5** | **Computer Vision & Autonomous Systems** | Tesla / Waymo sensor view (bounding boxes tracking pedestrians, cyclists, and lane markers). | **Speaker:** *"From self-driving electric cars to medical AI spotting tumors that human doctors miss—machine learning models process visual pixel data faster than human eyes."* |
| **Slide 6** | **The Dark Side: Bias, Hallucination & Deepfakes** | Side-by-side comparison: Hyper-realistic AI video of a celebrity vs real footage with subtle artifact cues. | **Speaker:** *"AI is only as good as the data it trains on. If data has biases, AI amplifies them. Furthermore, AI 'hallucinates'—it confidently makes up fake facts! And with deepfakes, we’ve entered an era where seeing is no longer believing."* |
| **Slide 7** | **Will AI Take Your Future Job?** | Quote: *"AI won't replace humans, but humans who know how to use AI will replace those who don't."* | **Speaker:** *"Whether you want to be a software engineer, doctor, lawyer, graphic designer, or entrepreneur, AI will be your co-pilot. Learning prompt engineering, data logic, and critical thinking is your greatest competitive edge."* |
| **Slide 8** | **Tech Careers of Tomorrow** | Clean grid: AI Ethics Officer, Cloud Architect, Cybersecurity Analyst, Bio-Tech Informatics, Game Engine Developer. | **Speaker:** *"University programs at UTM and top global institutions are evolving fast. You don't just have to code—tech needs UX researchers, product managers, cybersecurity defenders, and ethical policymakers."* |
| **Slide 9** | **Creator vs Consumer Mindset** | Split graphic: Passive thumb scrolling on phone vs hands typing on keyboard / designing prototypes. | **Speaker:** *"The world is divided into people who passively consume algorithms and people who build and leverage them. Which one do you want to be?"* |
| **Slide 10** | **Session Takeaway: Stay Curious, Stay Critical** | Bold slide: *"Question the output. Understand the system. Build the future."* | **Speaker:** *"Take that curiosity into our Fast-Answer Tech Showdown right now. Let’s see who has the fastest reflexes and sharpest mind!"* |

## Part 2: Interactive Workshop — “Be Smart Online: Cyber Resilience & Digital Footprint”
- **Duration:** 30 Minutes (10:15 – 10:45 AM)
- **Target Audience:** Teens aged 13–17
- **Tone:** Real, honest, urgent, respectful (no patronizing or condescending language).

### Slide-by-Slide Outline & Script

| Slide # | Slide Title | Visual / Media Suggestion | Core Lesson & Practical Takeaway |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Slide 1** | **Cyber Resilience: Defending Your Digital Identity** | Digital padlock with holographic biometric scan. | **Speaker:** *"To hackers and scammers, teenagers are prime targets: you have active digital footprints, gaming accounts with real value, and you trust online links. Today we discuss real digital security."* |
| **Slide 2** | **The Permanent Digital Footprint** | Screenshot mockup of a university scholarship interview: Interviewer pulling up candidate's old tweets/comments from 4 years ago. | **Speaker:** *"There is no true 'delete' on the internet. Screenshots, archived caches, and server logs exist forever. Universities, scholarship panels, and future employers already run background checks on social handles."* |
| **Slide 3** | **Password Cracking & Credential Stuffing** | Table showing time to crack: 8-char simple (`0.2 seconds`) vs 16-char passphrase (`200,000 years`). | Teach **Passphrase Strategy**: Combine 3–4 unrelated, memorable words with symbols: `Neon!Piano#Turtle44`.<br>**Critical Habit:** Never reuse the same password across Discord, Steam, and personal email. |
| **Slide 4** | **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA): Your Bulletproof Vest** | App icon: Google Authenticator / Microsoft Authenticator / YubiKey. | **Speaker:** *"Even if someone steals your password, 2FA stops them in their tracks. Always enable Authenticator App-based 2FA on your Steam, Discord, Instagram, and Google accounts."* |
| **Slide 5** | **The Social Engineering Traps: Case Studies** | Screenshots of Discord Nitro phishing, fake Steam trading bots, fake university grant SMS, and hacked friend DMs. | **Interactive breakdown of Red Flags:**<br>1. Manufactured urgency (*"Act within 15 minutes or get banned!"*)<br>2. Fake login URLs (e.g., `steam-communiity-gift.ru`)<br>3. Compromised friend accounts sending weird links. |
| **Slide 6** | **Deepfake Scams & AI Voice Cloning** | Audio wave graphic explaining 3-second voice cloning. | **Speaker:** *"Scammers only need a 3-second audio clip of your voice from an Instagram story to clone your voice and call your parents pretending you're in trouble asking for money. Establish a 'Family Secret Safe Word'!"* |
| **Slide 7** | **Online Privacy & Geolocation Hazards** | Photo of a student selfie in school uniform with a geotag and house background. | Highlight subtle leaks: School emblem on uniform, street signs in window reflection, boarding pass barcodes (contains full passport/ID data). |
| **Slide 8** | **Digital Blackmail & Online Exploitation** | Urgent warning shield with helpline icons (Cyber999, Talian Kasih). | **Serious Guidance:** Never send compromising private photos. If targeted by extortion or blackmail: **DO NOT PAY**. Scammers always ask for more. Immediately tell parents, school counselors, or report to MCMC/police. |
| **Slide 9** | **Trolling, Doxxing & Group Chat Culture** | Split graphic: Constructive gamer banter vs toxic doxxing (releasing private info). | **Speaker:** *"Group chats feel private, but screenshot leaks happen in seconds. Doxxing (releasing someone's home address, school, or phone number) is a criminal offense in many jurisdictions."* |
| **Slide 10** | **The Bystander Effect Online** | Graphic of a group chat where one student is being ganged up on. | Teach **Upstander Tactics**: Don't laugh or forward cruel memes. Send a private message checking in on the targeted friend. Report toxic posts. |
| **Slide 11** | **Interactive Table Dilemma: "The Compromised Admin"** | Scenario card displayed on screen. | **Scenario:** *"Your friend is an admin of a school study Discord server with 300 members. They click a 'beta tester' link for a game. Within 5 minutes, their account starts spamming crypto scams to all members and locking out other admins. What are the first 3 emergency actions?"* (3 minutes table discussion). |
| **Slide 12** | **The Modern Digital Citizen Code** | Clean 4-point manifesto. | 1. Lock down accounts with 2FA & unique passphrases.<br>2. Think before sharing personal data.<br>3. Verify suspicious links and media.<br>4. Stand up against online toxicity. |
