## 2. Table Mentor Playbook (Mentoring 13–17 Year Olds)

Teens respond best to mentors who treat them as equals and emerging young professionals rather than school pupils.

### Guiding Principles
- **Relatable & Approachable:** Introduce yourself with your name, university year, and major (e.g., *"Hey! I'm Alex, 2nd-year Software Engineering at UTM"*).
- **Foster Balanced Mixed-Gender Dynamics:**
  - Prevent one or two confident students (e.g., outspoken boys or dominant personalities) from overshadowing quieter teammates.
  - Explicitly encourage shared roles: Rotate responsibilities so that everyone contributes to the ideation, poster design, and stage pitch.
  - Set a table rule: **Both male and female co-founders must take the microphone during the 3-minute presentation.**
- **Facilitate, Don't Dictate:** Ask guiding questions rather than prescribing ideas. Help them stress-test their concepts.

### Milestone Coaching During the 45-Min Startup Sprint (10:45 – 11:30 AM)
- **Min 0 – 10 (Problem Framing & Ideation):**
  - Prompt: *"What is something in your daily school life, gaming, community, or home that is genuinely annoying or inefficient?"*
  - Help them narrow down to **one clear, high-impact problem**.
- **Min 10 – 25 (AI Architecture & UI Wireframing):**
  - Prompt: *"How does AI solve this better than a simple Excel sheet or Google search?"*
  - Assign roles:
    - 2 students design the mobile app UI screens / wireframe on sticky notes.
    - 2 students write the value proposition and core features.
    - 1–2 students outline the impact and business model.
- **Min 25 – 35 (Visual Polish & Poster Assembly):**
  - Make sure headings are bold, high-contrast, and legible from 10 feet away.
- **Min 35 – 45 (Pitch Rehearsal):**
  - Practice the 3-minute pitch twice with a phone stopwatch!
  - Coach on posture, voice projection, eye contact with the judges, and a memorable closing slogan.

## 3. Master Logistics & Technical Checklist

### Audio-Visual & Digital Setup
- [ ] **Dual Projectors / Main LED Screen:** Full HD/4K HDMI inputs for slides, videos, and Fast-Answer Quiz questions.
- [ ] **Microphones:** 2 wireless handheld mics for Emcee & audience questions; 1 wireless lapel mic for the keynote trainer.
- [ ] **Audio PA System:** Connected to laptop for music playlist (synthwave / lofi tech beats) and chime sound effects.

## 4. Teen Welfare & Sensitive Topic Protocols

### Digital Blackmail / Harassment Disclosures
During the Cyber Resilience session, discussions on online predators, sextortion, or severe cyberbullying may trigger personal disclosures from teens.
- **Protocol:**
  - Never interrogate or dismiss the student.
  - Discreetly connect the student to the **Lead Teacher / Accompanying School Counselor** in a private room.
  - Provide official Malaysian support resources if needed: **Cyber999 (CyberSecurity Malaysia)** hotlines (`1-300-88-2999`) or **Talian Kasih (`15999`)**.

### Fast-Answer Showdown Contingency
Since the quiz operates 100% device-free directly from the projector screen:
- **Zero Network Risk:** No dependency on Wi-Fi connectivity or student phone ownership.
- **Visual Clarity:** If lighting washes out screen contrast, Emcee reads the question and all 4 choices clearly twice.
- **Fairness Guarantee:** 3 roving spotters cover left, center, and right flanks to ensure fair recognition of the fastest hand raised.
