# 🎮 03: Interactive Activities, Fast-Answer Tech Showdown & Hands-On Guide (Teen Edition)

This guide contains updated high-engagement icebreakers, a 5-question fast-answer tech showdown calibrated for teens aged 13–17 (*Zero phone/device required — "Siapa Cepat, Dia Menang"*), and the "AI Startup Pitch Sprint" hands-on activity.

---

## 1. Ice Breaking Session (09:00 – 09:15 AM | 15 Mins)

### Objective
Break down initial awkwardness among mixed-gender teens, foster instant team chemistry, and set a lively, collegiate tone.

---

### Activity 1A: Teen Tech Charades (8 Mins)
- **Rules:** One representative from each table draws a secret prompt and acts it out silently (no speaking, no mouthing words) for 45 seconds. Their table gets 10 points if they guess correctly; if they fail, any other table can steal for 5 points!
- **Prompts Tailored for Teens:**
  1. **Realizing you sent a private screenshot to the person it was about:** Eyes wide, jaw dropping, scrambling frantically to hit "Unsend" before they read it.
  2. **Getting rejected by "I am not a robot" CAPTCHA 5 times:** Squinting closely at traffic lights, clicking furiously, slamming forehead against the desk.
  3. **Trying to record a TikTok dance in public when strangers walk past:** Starting with confident dance moves, immediately freezing awkwardly, pretending to check text messages.
  4. **The Discord Moderator on duty:** Sitting back smugly, adjusting imaginary glasses, aggressively hitting the imaginary "BAN" hammer with zero mercy.
  5. **AirDrop accident on a crowded bus:** Tapping phone casually, suddenly seeing someone accept a strange photo, turning red and hiding phone in pocket.
  6. **Phone dying at 1% while showing concert / festival ticket at the gate:** Tapping dead black screen, shaking phone, pleading with the ticket scanner.
  7. **High-ping / 999ms gaming lag during a clutch moment:** Smooth heroic shooting posture suddenly glitching, freezing in place, teleporting backward, and dying instantly.
  8. **Trying to remember a complex 16-character password you just changed yesterday:** Staring into space, pacing, trying 4 combinations, getting locked out for 30 minutes.

## 2. Fast-Answer Tech Showdown: “Siapa Cepat, Dia Menang!” (09:35 – 10:00 AM | 25 Mins)

Aktiviti kuiz pantas berimpak tinggi yang **100% bebas peranti (Zero Devices Needed)**. Murid tidak memerlukan telefon bimbit atau capaian internet. Semua soalan dan pilihan jawapan dipaparkan dengan visual menarik di skrin utama dewan.

### Peraturan & Format Permainan:
1. **Pancaran Skrin:** Ahli AV memaparkan soalan di skrin projektor. Emcee membacakan soalan dan 4 pilihan jawapan (A, B, C, D).
2. **Isyarat Masa:** Emcee memberi aba-aba: *"Tangan atas meja... 3, 2, 1... ANGKAT TANGAN!"*
3. **Spotter Pantas:** Emcee dan fasilitator lantai bertindak sebagai *spotter* bagi mengenal pasti murid atau meja yang mengangkat tangan paling pantas.
4. **Ganjaran Segera:**
   - **Jawapan Betul:** +20 Mata Meja + Cenderahati snek / stiker segera kepada murid penjawab.
   - **Jawapan Kurang Tepat:** Peluang dibuka kepada meja lain untuk *steal* (+10 Mata).
5. **Ringkasan Edukatif (30 saat):** Selepas setiap soalan dijawab, paparkan slaid penerangan ringkas agar murid faham konsep di sebalik teknologi tersebut.

---

### 5 Soalan Pilihan Pantas (Curated for 13–17 Years)

#### ❓ Soalan 1: Generative AI & LLMs
> **Situasi:** Korang minta ChatGPT buatkan esei sejarah, tetapi ChatGPT dengan yakin memberi tarikh dan nama tokoh perjuangan yang sebenarnya tidak pernah wujud dalam sejarah dunia. Dalam sains AI, fenomena rekaan fakta palsu ini dipanggil apa?
- **A)** AI Lagging / Freezing
- **B)** **AI Hallucination (Halusinasi AI)** *(JAWAPAN TEPAT)*
- **C)** AI Virus Infection
- **D)** Machine Fatigue  
*💡 Fakta Pantas Emcee: AI Generatif seperti ChatGPT tidak berfikir seperti manusia; ia meramal gabungan perkataan mengikut kebarangkalian statistik. Kadangkala ia 'berhalusinasi' mereka fakta dengan nada sangat meyakinkan! Sentiasa semak silang (cross-check) fakta sebenar.*

---

#### ❓ Soalan 2: Algoritma Media Sosial & FYP
> **Situasi:** Korang tengok satu video kucing comel di TikTok / Reels selama 30 saat. Tiba-tiba sepanjang minggu berikutnya, 80% feed korang dipenuhi video kucing. Kenapa ini berlaku?
- **A)** Kamera telefon mengimbas mata korang secara rahsia
- **B)** Kucing dalam video itu telah menggodam telefon korang
- **C)** **Algoritma Cadangan (Recommendation Algorithm) mengesan masa tontonan (watch-time) dan minat korang** *(JAWAPAN TEPAT)*
- **D)** Semua pengguna di seluruh dunia memang dapat video yang serupa  
*💡 Fakta Pantas Emcee: Algoritma media sosial mengoptimumkan 'Watch-Time' dan 'Engagement' untuk memastikan korang lekat dalam aplikasi selama mungkin. Ini boleh memerangkap kita dalam 'Filter Bubble' jika tidak dikawal.*

---

#### ❓ Soalan 3: Cyber Scam & Social Engineering
> **Situasi:** Korang terima mesej WhatsApp dari nombor misteri: *"Tahniah! Nombor telefon anda terpilih memenangi RM3,000 Touch 'n Go eWallet! Klik pautan pantas bit.ly ini dalam tempoh 10 minit sebelum hadiah hangus!"* Apakah tindakan PALING BIJAK?
- **A)** Klik segera sebab takut terlepas wang tunai RM3,000
- **B)** Forward mesej itu kepada group WhatsApp sekolah
- **C)** **Jangan klik langsung; sekat nombor (block) dan abaikan/laporkan mesej tersebut** *(JAWAPAN TEPAT)*
- **D)** Balas mesej dan minta mereka hantar wang tunai ke rumah  
*💡 Fakta Pantas Emcee: Taktik mencipta rasa cemas/tergesa-gesa (Urgency: 10 minit) berserta hadiah wang lumayan adalah ciri asas taktik penipuan Phishing! Sekali klik pautan palsu, data peribadi atau akaun boleh dicuri.*

---

#### ❓ Soalan 4: Deepfake & Keselamatan Visual
> **Situasi:** Muncul satu video tular di media sosial menunjukkan seorang personaliti terkenal mengajak orang ramai melabur skim cepat kaya. Suara dan mukanya kelihatan serupa, tetapi gerak bibirnya sedikit tidak seimbang dan matanya jarang berkelip. Apakah nama teknologi penciptaan video palsu berasaskan AI ini?
- **A)** **Deepfake (Synthetic AI Media)** *(JAWAPAN TEPAT)*
- **B)** Photoshop Express
- **C)** 3D Pixar Animation
- **D)** Blue-Screen Chroma Key  
*💡 Fakta Pantas Emcee: Deepfake dicipta menggunakan model kecerdasan buatan (Deep Learning) yang melatih ribuan gambar dan rakaman suara seseorang. Jangan mudah percaya klip sensasi tanpa mengesahkan sumber rasmi!*

---

#### ❓ Soalan 5: Jejak Digital (Digital Footprint)
> **Situasi:** Amir memuat naik video kecaman dan gambar sensitif di media sosialnya semasa marah. Sejam kemudian, dia menyesal dan menekan butang "DELETE POST". Adakah maklumat tersebut benar-benar hilang selama-lamanya dari dunia internet?
- **A)** Ya, sekali padam, terus lesap 100% dari internet
- **B)** **Tidak, pihak lain mungkin sudah membuat tangkap layar (screenshot) dan salinan log masih wujud di pelayan (server)** *(JAWAPAN TEPAT)*
- **C)** Ya, asalkan dia menggunakan telefon jenama iPhone
- **D)** Ya, asalkan dia log keluar dari akaun selepas padam  
*💡 Fakta Pantas Emcee: 'The Internet Never Forgets!' Jejak digital (Digital Footprint) kita adalah kekal. Kolej universiti berprestij dan majikan masa depan kini lazim menyemak sejarah media sosial calon sebelum menawarkan biasiswa atau pekerjaan!*

## 3. Hands-On Startup Sprint: “The AI Pitch Challenge” (10:45 – 11:30 AM | 45 Mins)

### Project Concept
Transform the table of 5–7 teens into a **Founding Tech Startup Team**. Their mission: Conceptualize an innovative AI-powered application or hardware device that tackles a real problem faced by teens, students, or their local community.

### Suggested Startup Prompt Themes
1. **StudyFlow AI:** An adaptive learning assistant that diagnoses why a student struggles with Physics/Math, formats concepts into personalized analogies, and prevents study burnout.
2. **ScamShield Extension:** A browser & messaging plugin powered by AI that detects deceptive phishing patterns, deepfake audio, and predatory messages in real time before users click.
3. **GreenCampus Drone & Sorter:** An automated smart recycling system using computer vision to detect, sort, and compress recyclables in schools.
4. **MentalHealth Companion (SafeSpace):** An empathetic, privacy-focused AI conversational coach trained in youth mindfulness, offering de-escalation tips and connecting to certified human counselors when needed.
5. **SkillSwap Youth Marketplace:** An AI-curated talent exchange where teens barter skills (e.g., teaching video editing in exchange for coding tuition or guitar lessons) safely with verified peer badges.

## 4. Startup Pitch Showcase & Judging (11:30 – 11:50 AM | 20 Mins)

### Pitch Rules
- **Pitch Duration:** Maximum 3 minutes per startup (strictly enforced by audio chime).
- **Pitch Structure:**
  - *0:00 – 0:45:* The Hook & The Problem (*"Raise your hand if you've ever..."*)
  - *0:45 – 2:00:* The AI Solution & Poster UI Walkthrough
  - *2:00 – 2:45:* Target Impact & Sustainability
  - *2:45 – 3:00:* Final Call-to-Action / Slogan
- **Team Inclusivity:** Both male and female team members must take active speaking roles.

### Honorary Awards & Judging Categories
> **Nota:** Semua anugerah adalah **Gelaran Kehormat (Honorary Titles)** sahaja — tiada hadiah fizikal. Pengiktirafan melalui pengumuman dramatik di pentas, tepukan gemuruh, dan sesi foto khas pemenang bersama poster mereka.

1. 🦄 **The Unicorn Award (Best Overall Startup Innovation):** The most creative and technically plausible AI application.
2. 🎨 **Best UX & Visual Pitch Deck:** The most beautifully organized, clear, and engaging Mahjong paper prototype.
3. 🎤 **Venture Pitch Masters (Best Stage Delivery):** The team with the most confident delivery, charisma, and public speaking flair.
4. 🛡️ **Cyber Ethics & Social Impact Champion:** The startup addressing the most urgent social, ethical, or safety challenge in modern society.
5. ⚙️ **Technical Feasibility Award:** The team that best articulated *how* the underlying AI/machine learning technology actually works.
